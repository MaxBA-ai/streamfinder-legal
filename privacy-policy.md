# Privacy Policy for StreamScout

**Last Updated:** April 15, 2026

## Introduction

StreamScout ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

## Information We Do Not Collect

StreamScout does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

- **No account or login required.** The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, crash reporters, or third-party tracking tools.
- **No location data.** The app does not access your device's GPS, IP-based location, or any other location services.
- **No device identifiers.** The app does not collect or transmit device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data.
- **No cookies or web tracking.** The app does not use cookies or similar technologies.

## Data Stored on Your Device

StreamScout stores the following data **locally on your device only** using AsyncStorage:

- **Country preference:** The country you select for filtering streaming availability (e.g., "United States").
- **Favorite providers:** Your preferred streaming service selections.
- **Favorites list:** Movies and TV shows you have saved to your favorites.

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server. If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted.

## Third-Party API Services

StreamScout communicates with two external services to provide movie and streaming data:

### TMDB (The Movie Database)
- Website: https://www.themoviedb.org
- Data sent: Search queries (movie/show titles), content IDs for detail lookups
- Data received: Movie/TV show metadata, poster images, streaming provider availability, trending content
- Privacy policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

### OMDb (Open Movie Database)
- Website: https://www.omdbapi.com
- Data sent: IMDb IDs or movie titles for rating lookups
- Data received: IMDb ratings, Rotten Tomatoes ratings
- Privacy policy: https://www.omdbapi.com/privacy.htm

These services receive only the search queries and content identifiers necessary to fulfill your requests. No personal information is included in these requests.

### YouTube (via External Browser)

When you tap "Watch Trailer," the app opens a YouTube link in your device's default browser or the YouTube app. StreamScout does not send any data to YouTube directly; the link is opened externally.

## VPN Suggestions

StreamScout displays information about which countries have streaming availability for a given title. This is informational only. The app does **not** provide VPN services, does **not** connect to VPN servers, and does **not** facilitate VPN usage. Any decision to use a VPN is made independently by the user.

## Children's Privacy

StreamScout does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect information from any user regardless of age.

## Data Security

Since StreamScout does not collect or transmit personal data, there is minimal data security risk. Local preferences stored via AsyncStorage are protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue at: https://github.com/MaxBA-ai/StreamScout/issues

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. Your locally stored preferences can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings.
